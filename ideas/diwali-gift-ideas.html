7 Personalised Diwali Gift Ideas for Family, Friends and Colleagues

A small printed touch makes a Diwali gift feel personal. Here are seven ideas we design and print in Goa and deliver across India, usually within 5 to 6 days.

1. Diya gift tags for every box of sweets

Tie a colourful Happy Diwali tag to each box of mithai or dry fruits. It is the quickest way to make a bulk gift look thoughtful, and we can add your family name or company name.

2. Nine Goddesses tags for family gifts

Our Shubh Deepawali tags feature nine goddess designs at 3 by 2.5 inches, a lovely fit for gifts to elders and for pooja thalis.

3. Mini greeting cards instead of forwarded messages

A set of twelve mini Diwali cards lets you write a few words to each neighbour, teacher or colleague. A handwritten note stands out when every phone is full of forwards.

4. Diwali bookmarks for readers and students

Bookmarks with diyas, lanterns and lotus designs make an easy gift for students, book clubs and teachers, and they last long after the festival.

5. Printed money envelopes for shagun

Give Diwali cash gifts in a printed envelope with a matching tag. We can design envelopes with the recipient's name or a family message.

6. Fridge magnets as a gift that stays

A custom fridge magnet with a blessing, a family photo or your shop's logo is seen every day of the year, which makes it a smart corporate Diwali gift too.

7. Return gifts for Diwali parties and kids

Hosting a Diwali party or a school celebration? We make return gifts with printed thank-you tags, so every guest leaves with something to remember.
