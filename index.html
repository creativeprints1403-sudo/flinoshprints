About Flinosh Prints

Flinosh Prints is a design and print studio based in Goa that delivers across India. It began in April 2026 with a request from a family member: a digital invite for her son's 7th birthday. I made it just for my nephew, but the joy it brought turned a random idea into a business of designing and printing.

Today we design and print cards, invitations, envelopes, gift tags, bookmarks, fridge magnets and return gifts, each made to feel as personal as that first invite. We take orders directly, including local orders, and also sell on Amazon for secure checkout and tracked delivery across India.

FAQ

Questions people ask

Do you take local orders in Goa?

Yes. Flinosh Prints is a registered business in Goa, and we take local orders for invitations, cards, gift tags, return gifts and more. WhatsApp, call or email us with your idea.

Do you deliver across India?

Yes. We are based in Goa and deliver all over India. Order directly on WhatsApp, or buy on Amazon.in for secure checkout and tracked delivery.

Can you customise a design with my names, photo or theme?

Yes. Every piece is designed by us, so we can add names, dates, photos, colours or a theme of your choice. Message us on WhatsApp with your idea.

How do I place an order?

WhatsApp, call or email us to order directly, or order on Amazon.in and add your details in the customisation box.

Do you take bulk orders for schools, events and businesses?

Yes. We print in bulk for schools, playschools, weddings, parties and businesses, including educational charts, name tags, invitations, return gift tags and visiting cards.

How much do your products cost?

Prices depend on the design, size and quantity. Message us on WhatsApp or email us with what you need and we will send you a price. Amazon listings show their prices on Amazon.in.

How long does an order take?

Standard delivery takes 5 to 6 days. Large or fully custom orders can take longer, so tell us when you need it and we will confirm a delivery date before we start printing.

What about returns and refunds?

Orders placed on Amazon follow Amazon's return policy. For orders placed directly with us, we confirm the design with you before printing, and if anything is wrong with your order, contact us and we will make it right.

Do you design logos and visiting cards?

Yes. We design logos, visiting cards, labels and stickers, and can print them for you too.
