About Flinosh Prints

Flinosh Prints began in April 2026 with a request from a family member: a digital invite for her son's 7th birthday. I made it just for my nephew, but the joy it brought turned a random idea into a business of designing and printing.

Today we design and print cards, invitations, envelopes, gift tags, bookmarks, fridge magnets and return gifts, each made to feel as personal as that first invite. We sell through Amazon for secure checkout and tracked delivery, and we ship across India.
